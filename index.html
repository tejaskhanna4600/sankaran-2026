Who We Are

VSE's Premier Academic Festival

Sankaran is the flagship annual academic fest of Arthvidya – VIPS Club of Economists, organised under the Vivekananda School of Economics (VSE) at Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies-TC, Pitampura, Delhi.

A two-day intercollege festival brimming with Economics, Business, Finance, strategy and creative competitions — drawing the sharpest minds from colleges across Delhi NCR. With cash prizes and rewards worth ₹40,000+, Sankaran Ver.2026 is the biggest edition yet.
